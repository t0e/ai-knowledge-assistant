# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## 1. Annual Leave and Vacation Policy
Full-time permanent employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 10 unused leave days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year, but must be utilized before March 31st. Unused rollover days beyond this deadline are forfeited without monetary compensation. Part-time employees receive prorated leave proportional to their contracted hours.

## 2. Remote Work and Equipment Allowances
Acme Corp operates a remote-first workplace model. All eligible full-time employees receive a one-time $1,000 home office setup allowance upon completing their 90-day probationary period. Additionally, employees receive a $50 monthly recurring stipend for high-speed internet reimbursement. Contractors and temporary workers are not eligible for the home office equipment allowance.

## 3. Performance Reviews and Promotion Cycles
Performance evaluations are conducted on a quarterly cadence in March, June, September, and December. Annual salary adjustments and discretionary performance bonuses are determined during the Q4 review cycle based on individual KPI completion and company revenue milestones.

## 4. Code of Conduct and Conflict Resolution
Employees are expected to uphold the highest standards of professional integrity and mutual respect. Any instances of workplace harassment or discrimination must be reported immediately to Human Resources via hr@acmecorp.example or through the anonymous compliance hotline.
